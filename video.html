About our videos

Rhizomium's accent colour changed from lime to rust orange. Our videos and screenshots were all recorded with the old lime look.

The recoloured videos were not real recordings

The change was made in a cloud environment without a graphics card, so the app could not be re-recorded. Instead our AI coding assistant digitally altered the old lime footage and screenshots to look rust orange, and they were shown as if they were the app.

They were not: they were edited images of an older build, not captures of the current one. That was wrong, and those videos are being removed.

Why YouTube has no new videos

Uploading to YouTube from our tools needs Google Cloud services, and those are not available for our account. We could not replace the old lime videos there yet. Until real recordings of the current app can be uploaded, this page stands in for the channel.
