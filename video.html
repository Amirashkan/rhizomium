About our videos

Rhizomium's accent colour changed from lime to rust orange. Our videos and screenshots were all recorded with the old lime look.

Why the videos look slightly off

Re-recording needs a machine with a real graphics card, and the change was made in a cloud environment without one. So instead of re-recording, the lime in the existing footage was recoloured automatically, pixel by pixel, by our AI coding assistant.

It mostly works, but it is not a real capture: you may spot a faint green edge or a slightly shifted colour here and there. That is the recolouring, not the app.

Why there is no YouTube channel

Uploading to YouTube from our tools needs Google Cloud services, and those are not available for our account. We could not replace the old lime videos there, so the channel is being closed.
